Services

Two ways to work together

Fixed scope, scoped price on a discovery call — or ongoing capacity if you need it maintained and extended.

Custom Data Pipeline & Automation Build
$3,000–$6,000 · scoped on a call · 5 business days

One pipeline, one data source, production-grade from day one — not a prototype. Most single-source builds land $3,500–$4,000; price is fixed before work starts, not hourly.

  • Idempotent ingestion — safe to re-run without duplicating data
  • Schema validation with explicit quality gates
  • Dead-letter queue for records that fail validation, instead of silently dropping them
  • Delivery in your format — Parquet, PDF, or dashboard
  • Documentation so your team can maintain it
Out of scope at this tier: multi-source orchestration, ML modeling, ongoing maintenance — see the partner tier for that.
Embedded AI & Data Systems Partner
From $2,500/month · scoped in the discovery call

Ongoing ownership of what's already built, plus new pipelines as they come up. Engineering capacity, scoped and billed like engineering capacity — not a generic AI-strategy retainer.

  • SLO-based monitoring with error-budget burn-rate alerting — not just up/down
  • RBAC with an immutable audit trail where the data needs access control
  • CI/CD so nothing ships without tests passing
  • New automation builds as needs arise
  • Direct access via message for ad hoc data pulls or fixes
Cancel anytime with 7 days' notice. No long-term commitment.
Custom Research & Outreach Agents
Scoped on a call

An agent that does the work of a full-time researcher/SDR: finds people or companies matching your exact criteria, researches each one, scores fit, and drafts the first outreach message — you review and send. Built the same way as Jack, the agent I run for my own sales pipeline.

  • Defined fit rubric, not a black-box score
  • Research summary per candidate, not just a name and a link
  • Personalized outreach drafts, ready to review
  • A tracked pipeline file, not a spreadsheet you maintain by hand
Out of scope: fully autonomous sending. You stay in the loop on every message that goes out.
