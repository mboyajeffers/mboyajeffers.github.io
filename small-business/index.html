Backend Setup

Backend Fix-It
$500–$1,500 one-time · scoped on a call

You already have tools — they just don't talk to each other, or something's quietly broken. I find what's actually wrong and fix it.

  • Full audit of what's currently in place

  • A plain-language writeup of what was wrong and what changed

Monthly Operations Care
$300–$800/month · scoped on a call

  • New connections added as your tools change
